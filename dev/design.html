Design language preview

One island, one look.

Every colour, size, curve and part the product is built from. The island, its sections, the floating windows and settings are dark everywhere and share one set of tokens and one set of components.

Mascot candidates

Three original directions, drawn in Canvas 2D at the display's pixel ratio. Each takes the pill colour, follows the pointer, and has to stay readable at 22 px on the island's black. The controls drive the left three sizes of every candidate at once; the strip on the right holds each state still at 28 px.
