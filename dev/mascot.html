Mascot and sounds

The Visor (ARCHITECTURE §9) and the 29 synthesised sounds (§10). Everything here is the shipping code: src/mascot/engine.ts, greeting.ts, src/upload/canvas.ts, src/recap/share.ts, src/core/synth.ts and the icons from scripts/gen-icons.mjs.

Live

Move the pointer: the bar follows it. Rest on a mascot for 1.9 s: love. Click one: it looks annoyed and shakes; three clicks inside 1.7 s make it dizzy for 3.3 s.

Every emote

Each one replays every 2.8 s. Mini pills (22 px) are the body and the bar only: no hearts, no sparkle.

Greeting

1.6 s: the strip slides down, the body rises from below on a 0.5 s spring and blinks twice, with the greeting motif.

Drop sequence

The band opens into a hatch, the file slides in behind the lower lip, and the mascot rides the progress bar. Replays every 12 s (open it alone).
